# Carriage illustrations

Five original transparent PNG illustrations, generated for this project with the built-in image generation tool on 12 November 2026. Each file is **1774 × 887 pixels**, RGBA. Total download is approximately 16.3 MB (15.5 MiB). Files are local, without third-party image/CDN requests. The app decodes each image once and caches its visible bounds. Originals remain intact; transparent margins are cropped only when composing the train.

Floor796 was inspected as a reference for populated scenes, depth and detail density. None of its artwork, characters or source files are included. These are generated illustrations, not hand-pixelled animation. People, wheels and objects in these PNGs are static; the application animates the surrounding journey and steam separately. Enlarging beyond 100% does not add new source detail.

## Generation briefs

The following records the visual briefs used, condensed for reuse rather than a verbatim generation log. The library was generated first; it was then supplied as a structural reference for the four other images.

Common direction: a highly detailed inhabited railway carriage seen side-on as a cutaway miniature world; coherent shallow spatial depth, crisp small objects, visible people performing different tasks, dark teal and brass exterior, warm interiors, transparent background, two bogies and couplings in consistent positions. A complete standalone sprite, with no page, typography, UI, logo or surrounding landscape. Prioritize readable scenes and meaningful objects over texture noise.

- **library.png:** a two-storey travelling library, mezzanine and spiral stairs, densely filled bookshelves, fireplace, globe and maps, readers, chess players, librarian, tea service, a small book-carrying robot and a sleeping cat. Warm timber, brass lamps and varied clusters of activity.
- **greenhouse.png:** retain the library's shell and wheel layout; transform the interior into a glass-roof botanical carriage. Include gardeners, a potting bench, plant labels, tools, soil sacks, irrigation pipes, hanging plants, climbing vines, flowers, a small helper robot and a hammock. Lots of varied foliage without hiding the characters.
- **koi.png:** retain the carriage structure; an inhabited Japanese garden with koi pond, stepping stones, a small bridge, tea corner, maple canopy, moss, lanterns and carefully placed stones. Visitors and gardeners engage with the space. Warm red foliage, jade water and a dark teal/brass frame.
- **observatory.png:** retain the structure; a richly equipped mobile astronomical observatory with a large telescope and opening dome, scientists, star charts, brass instruments, orrery, cabinets, consoles, scattered papers and books. Deep blue and warm gold lighting, clear working areas and distinct activities.
- **engine.png:** a locomotive facing left that belongs to the same teal/brass train. Highly detailed boiler, rivets, piping, gears, wheels and mechanical chassis; side cutaway cabin with four crew members, controls and instruments. Match the carriage scale and rear coupling, isolated on transparency.
